Kaju Curry Recipe

Kaju curry is a restaurant style delicious recipe of a creamy kaju butter masala with a rich, tangy, sweet flavorful gravy made with cashews, tomatoes, cream and spices. Kaju is the Hindi word for cashews. This recipe of kaju masala has an overload of cashews as they are added twice during the cooking process. Serve with roti, naan, paratha or steamed rice for a sumptuous and filling lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cashew nuts
  • 2 medium onions
  • 3 medium tomatoes
  • 5 whole cashews
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 teaspoon ghee
  • 2 teaspoons cooking oil
  • ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 dried bay leaf
  • 2 green chilies
  • ½ tablespoon Kashmiri red chili powder
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala powder
  • ¼ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon kasuri methi
  • 1 tablespoon heavy cream
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Toast the cashews: Heat 1 teaspoon of ghee in a pan and fry 1 cup of cashews on low heat until they turn golden brown, then set them aside.
  2. Boil the base: In the same pan, add 2 teaspoons of oil and sauté 2 chopped onions, 3 chopped tomatoes, and 5 whole cashews until the tomatoes soften.
  3. Blend the paste: Let the mixture cool completely, then blend it into a smooth, fine paste without adding too much water.
  4. Sauté aromatics: Melt 1 tablespoon of butter in the pan, add 1 dried bay leaf and ½ teaspoon of cumin seeds, and let them crackle.
  5. Cook the ginger-garlic: Add 1 teaspoon of ginger-garlic paste and 2 slit green chilies, and sauté for 1 minute until the raw smell disappears.
  6. Simmer the gravy: Pour in the blended tomato-onion paste, mix well, and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until oil starts separating from the sides.
  7. Add the spices: Stir in ½ tablespoon of Kashmiri red chili powder, ½ teaspoon of garam masala, ¼ teaspoon of turmeric, 1 teaspoon of sugar, and salt to taste.
  8. Combine everything: Pour in ½ cup of water to adjust the consistency, bring it to a boil, and add the fried cashews.
  9. Finish the dish: Simmer for 2 minutes, then stir in 1 teaspoon of crushed kasuri methi and 1 tablespoon of heavy cream.
  10. Garnish and serve: Turn off the heat, garnish with fresh chopped cilantro, and serve hot with naan or roti.
